📲 Installation Instructions

After choosing an app above, follow the instructions for your phone or tablet.

  1. Tap the app you want above.
  2. Make sure its installer page is open in the Chrome app.
  3. Tap Chrome's three-dot menu.
  4. Tap Add page to.
  5. Choose Install as web app.
  6. Confirm the installation.
  7. Open the new app from your Home screen or Apps screen.
